# Deposits, Withdrawals, and Fees

## Overview

- This document covers how to add and remove funds from your brokerage account, supported payment methods, and all applicable fees.
- For customers who need to fund their account, withdraw profits, or understand the cost of trading.
- Knowing how money moves in and out of your account, and what fees apply, helps you avoid surprises and plan your trading costs.

## Key Concepts

- **Deposit**: Adding funds from your bank account, card, or digital wallet into your brokerage account.
- **Withdrawal**: Transferring funds from your brokerage account back to your bank account or original payment method.
- **Available Balance**: The amount of money you can use for trading or withdraw. This excludes funds tied up in open positions or pending transactions.
- **Commission**: A fixed fee charged per trade (e.g., $0 for stocks, $1.50 per options contract).
- **Spread**: The difference between the buy and sell price of an instrument. This is an implicit trading cost, especially relevant for forex and CFDs.
- **Overnight Fee (Swap)**: A daily charge applied to CFD and leveraged forex positions held past the end of the trading day.
- **Inactivity Fee**: A monthly fee charged if no trades are placed for 12 consecutive months.
- **Withdrawal Fee**: A fee that may apply depending on the withdrawal method and currency.

## How It Works

### Depositing Funds

1. Log in to your verified account.
2. Go to **Funds > Deposit**.
3. Select your payment method.
4. Enter the amount (minimum $100 for first deposit, $10 for subsequent deposits).
5. Confirm the transaction.
6. Funds are credited based on the method used (see processing times below).

### Supported Deposit Methods and Processing Times

| Method | Processing Time | Fee | Minimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank Transfer (ACH) | 1-3 business days | Free | $100 |
| Wire Transfer | Same day | $0 incoming / $25 outgoing | $500 |

| Credit Card | Instant | 1.5% | $10 |
| Digital Wallet (PayPal, Skrill) | Instant to 1 hour | Free | $10 |
| Cryptocurrency | Network confirmation time | Network fee only | $50 equivalent |

### Withdrawing Funds

1. Go to **Funds > Withdraw**.
2. Select the withdrawal method (must match a previously used deposit method for security).
3. Enter the amount.
4. Confirm via email or two-factor authentication.
5. Funds are processed based on the method (see below).

## Common Questions

- **Q: How do I deposit money into my account?**
  A: Go to **Funds > Deposit**, choose your payment method, enter the amount, and confirm. Most methods are instant or same-day.

- **Q: Why is my deposit not showing in my account?**
  A: Bank transfers take 1-3 business days to process. Card and wallet deposits should appear within minutes. Check **Funds > History** for the status.

- **Q: Can I withdraw to a different bank account than the one I deposited from?**
  A: For security, your first withdrawal must go to a previously used deposit method. To add a new withdrawal destination, go to **Settings > Payment Methods** and complete the verification process.

- **Q: Is there a maximum withdrawal amount?**
  A: There is no hard maximum, but withdrawals above $50,000 may require additional verification and take longer to process.

- **Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
  A: No. All fees are listed on our **Pricing** page and in the fee schedule above. The main costs are spreads on forex/CFDs, commissions on options, and the overnight fee for leveraged positions.

- **Q: What is the inactivity fee and how do I avoid it?**
  A: A $10/month fee is charged if you place no trades for 12 consecutive months. Any single trade resets the counter.

- **Q: Why was I charged a currency conversion fee?**
  A: If your account base currency is USD and you trade an instrument priced in EUR, the platform converts the currency and applies a 0.5% conversion fee. You can avoid this by opening a multi-currency account.

- **Q: Can I deposit using cryptocurrency?**
  A: Yes. Go to **Funds > Deposit > Crypto** and select the supported cryptocurrency. The minimum is $50 equivalent and processing depends on network confirmation times.

## Common Issues and Resolutions

- **Issue:** Deposit not credited after expected processing time
  - **Possible Causes:** Bank transfer still pending; payment declined by card issuer; incorrect reference number on wire transfer; crypto transaction awaiting network confirmations.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Check status under **Funds > History**. 2) Verify the transaction on your bank or card statement. 3) For wire transfers, confirm the reference number matches. 4) For crypto, check the transaction hash on a block explorer.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the funds have left the customer's bank/wallet but are not reflected after the maximum processing time for that method.

- **Issue:** Withdrawal request stuck in "Processing" status
  - **Possible Causes:** Pending identity re-verification; withdrawal exceeds daily limit; open positions using required margin; compliance review for large amounts.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Check **Funds > History** for any status messages. 2) Ensure KYC is fully up to date. 3) Verify that available balance (not total balance) covers the withdrawal amount. 4) Close positions if margin is locking your funds.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the withdrawal has been in "Processing" for more than 5 business days with no status update.

- **Issue:** Unexpected fee charged on account
  - **Possible Causes:** Overnight fee on a leveraged position; inactivity fee after 12 months; currency conversion fee; credit card surcharge.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Check the fee description under **Funds > History > Fees**. 2) Compare against the fee schedule above. 3) For overnight fees, review which positions were held past the daily cutoff.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the fee does not match any published rate or the customer believes it was applied in error.

- **Issue:** Payment method declined
  - **Possible Causes:** Insufficient funds in bank account; card expired or blocked for international transactions; bank flagging the transaction as suspicious.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Try a different payment method. 2) Contact your bank to authorize the transaction. 3) Ensure the card details are correct and not expired.
  - **When to Escalate:** If all payment methods fail and the customer's bank confirms no block on their end.

- **Issue:** Cannot add a new withdrawal method
  - **Possible Causes:** The new method has not been used for a deposit; additional verification required; account-level restriction.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Make a small deposit using the new method first. 2) Complete any additional verification prompts. 3) Check for account restrictions under **Settings > Account Status**.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the customer has completed verification but the system still does not allow the new method.

## Escalation and Support Routing

- **Do not handle:** Missing deposits where funds have left the customer's bank, withdrawal requests delayed beyond 5 business days, disputed fees, or refund requests.
- **Route to Payments Support:** Failed deposits, stuck withdrawals, payment method issues, fee disputes.
- **Route to Compliance Team:** Large withdrawal reviews, re-verification requests, account-level payment restrictions.
- **Information to collect before escalation:** Transaction reference or ID, amount and currency, payment method used, date of the transaction, screenshots of bank statements or error messages if available.
