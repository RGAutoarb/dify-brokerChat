# Accounts and Onboarding

## Overview

- This document covers account types offered by the brokerage, account opening steps, and identity verification (KYC).
- For anyone looking to open a new account or understand the differences between account types.
- Choosing the right account type and completing verification are the first steps to trading.

## Key Concepts

- **Cash Account**: A standard brokerage account where you trade using only the funds you deposit. No borrowing is involved.
- **Margin Account**: An account that allows you to borrow funds from the broker to trade larger positions. Requires additional approval and carries higher risk.
- **Demo Account**: A practice account with virtual funds so you can learn the platform without risking real money.
- **KYC (Know Your Customer)**: A legal requirement for brokers to verify your identity before you can deposit or trade. This protects you and prevents fraud.
- **Accredited Investor**: A classification for individuals who meet certain income or net worth thresholds, granting access to additional products.

## How It Works

### Opening an Account

1. Visit the registration page and enter your email address and a secure password.
2. Select the account type you want (Cash, Margin, or Demo).
3. Provide your personal information: full legal name, date of birth, address, and tax identification number.
4. Upload identity documents for KYC verification (government-issued photo ID and proof of address).
5. Answer a short suitability questionnaire about your trading experience and financial situation.
6. Review and accept the terms of service, risk disclosures, and privacy policy.
7. Wait for KYC review (typically 1-2 business days).
8. Once approved, fund your account and begin trading.

### Upgrading from Cash to Margin

1. Log in and navigate to **Settings > Account Type**.
2. Select **Apply for Margin Account**.
3. Complete the additional risk assessment questionnaire.
4. Review and sign the margin agreement.
5. Approval is typically processed within 1 business day.

## Common Questions

- **Q: What is the minimum deposit to open an account?**
  A: The minimum deposit for a Cash Account is $100. Margin Accounts require a minimum of $2,000 as required by regulation.

- **Q: Can I have more than one account?**
  A: Yes. You can hold one of each account type (Cash, Margin, Demo) under the same email address.

- **Q: How long does KYC verification take?**
  A: Most verifications complete within 24 hours. Complex cases may take up to 3 business days.

- **Q: What documents are accepted for KYC?**
  A: Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card) and a proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, bank statement, or government letter).

- **Q: Can I trade while my KYC is pending?**
  A: No. You must be fully verified before you can deposit funds or place trades.

- **Q: What is the difference between a cash and margin account?**
  A: A cash account only lets you trade with deposited funds. A margin account lets you borrow money from the broker to take larger positions, but this increases your risk.

- **Q: Can I downgrade from a margin account back to cash?**
  A: Yes. Close all margin positions first, then request a downgrade through **Settings > Account Type**.

- **Q: Is my Demo Account balance real money?**
  A: No. Demo accounts use virtual funds for practice only. Profits and losses are simulated.

## Common Issues and Resolutions

- **Issue:** KYC verification rejected
  - **Possible Causes:** Blurry or expired document; name mismatch between documents; unsupported document type.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Check the rejection reason in the notification email. 2) Re-upload a clear, valid document. 3) Ensure the name on your ID matches the name on your account exactly.
  - **When to Escalate:** If verification fails after two re-submissions, or if the customer believes the rejection is an error.

- **Issue:** Cannot log in to account
  - **Possible Causes:** Incorrect password; account locked after multiple failed attempts; email not confirmed.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Use the **Forgot Password** link to reset. 2) Check your email (including spam) for a confirmation link. 3) Wait 30 minutes if the account is temporarily locked.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the customer still cannot log in after resetting their password and confirming their email.

- **Issue:** Margin account application denied
  - **Possible Causes:** Insufficient trading experience reported on questionnaire; account balance below $2,000 minimum; failed suitability assessment.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Review the denial reason in the notification. 2) Ensure the account is funded with at least $2,000. 3) Re-apply after gaining more experience or updating your financial profile.
  - **When to Escalate:** If the customer meets all requirements and still receives a denial.

- **Issue:** Account shows incorrect personal information
  - **Possible Causes:** Typo during registration; outdated address after a move.
  - **Resolution Steps:** 1) Go to **Settings > Personal Information**. 2) Update address, phone number, or email directly. 3) Name or date of birth changes require submitting a support request with updated ID.
  - **When to Escalate:** Any request to change the legal name or date of birth on the account.

## Escalation and Support Routing

- **Do not handle:** Name or date-of-birth changes, suspected identity theft, regulatory holds, or tax-related account questions.
- **Route to KYC Team:** Repeated verification failures, document disputes, sanctions screening issues.
- **Route to Account Support:** Login issues unresolved after password reset, account closure requests, duplicate account merges.
- **Information to collect before escalation:** Full name on account, registered email address, description of the issue, any error messages or rejection reasons received.
