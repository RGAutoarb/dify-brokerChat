# Deposits, Withdrawals, and Fees — Sahel Capital Markets (SCM)

## Overview

This document covers how to add and remove funds from your Sahel Capital Markets (SCM) investment account, supported payment methods, and all applicable fees. SCM is a West African brokerage offering equities and bonds across the GSE, NSE, and BRVM. If you need to fund your account, withdraw proceeds, or understand the cost of investing, start here.

## Key Financial Concepts at SCM

### Available Balance and Settlement Period

Your available balance is the amount of money you can use for investing or withdraw from your SCM account. This excludes funds tied up in unsettled trades or pending transactions. When you sell shares, the proceeds are not available immediately due to the settlement period. GSE and BRVM equity sales settle in T+3 (3 business days after the trade). NSE equity sales settle in T+2. Once settlement is complete, proceeds move to your available balance.

### Dividends and Coupon Payments

When a company you hold shares in pays a dividend, the cash is automatically credited to your SCM account on the payment date at no charge. You must have held the shares on or before the ex-dividend date to be eligible. For bonds, coupon (interest) payments are credited automatically to your account on the scheduled payment dates at no charge. For example, the Ghana Government 3-Year Treasury Note pays coupons semi-annually.

### Currency Conversion at SCM

A 1.0% currency conversion fee applies when you trade an instrument denominated in a different currency from your account wallet. For example, if your account is in GHS and you buy shares on the NSE (priced in NGN) or the BRVM (priced in XOF), the conversion fee is charged. You can avoid this by adding a multi-currency wallet in the relevant currency under Settings then Currency Wallets.

## How to Deposit Funds into Your SCM Account

To deposit funds into your SCM Invest account, log in to your verified account and go to Funds then Deposit. Select your payment method, enter the amount (minimum GHS 50 for mobile money, GHS 100 for bank transfer), and confirm. For mobile money, approve the payment prompt on your phone. For bank transfers, use the reference number provided by SCM.

### Mobile Money Deposits at SCM

SCM supports instant, free deposits via mobile money. This is the fastest way to fund your SCM investment account.

MTN MoMo: Instant processing, free, minimum GHS 50.
Vodafone Cash: Instant processing, free, minimum GHS 50.
AirtelTigo Money: Instant processing, free, minimum GHS 50.

### Bank Transfer and Wire Deposits at SCM

SCM accepts bank transfers in GHS, NGN, and international currencies for funding your investment account.

Bank Transfer in GHS: 1-2 business days, free, minimum GHS 100.
Bank Transfer in NGN: 2-3 business days, free, minimum NGN 10,000.
International Wire in USD, EUR, or GBP: 2-3 business days, $15 flat fee, minimum $100 equivalent.
Cheque Deposit: 5-7 business days, free, minimum GHS 500.

## How to Withdraw Funds from Your SCM Account

To withdraw funds from your SCM Invest account, go to Funds then Withdraw. Select the withdrawal method (mobile money or bank transfer), enter the amount, and confirm via SMS OTP or two-factor authentication. Ensure the amount does not exceed your available balance — unsettled trade proceeds are not available for withdrawal until settlement is complete.

### Withdrawal Methods, Limits, and Processing Times at SCM

SCM supports the following withdrawal methods for your investment account. There is no limit on how many times you can withdraw per month, but mobile money withdrawal fees depend on frequency.

Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo): Same-day processing. You can make up to 2 free mobile money withdrawals per month. After 2 free withdrawals, each additional mobile money withdrawal costs GHS 2. There is no maximum number of withdrawals — you can withdraw as many times as you need.
Bank Transfer in GHS: 1-3 business days, GHS 2 flat fee per withdrawal, no frequency limit.
Bank Transfer in NGN: 2-4 business days, NGN 500 flat fee per withdrawal, no frequency limit.
International Wire in USD, EUR, or GBP: 3-5 business days, $25 flat fee per withdrawal, no frequency limit.

## SCM Fee Schedule: Equity Trading

SCM charges a 1.5% commission on the value of each equity trade (buying or selling shares), with a minimum of GHS 5 per trade. This applies to all 20 equities across the GSE, NSE, and BRVM. For example, buying GHS 1,000 worth of Consolidated Bank Ghana shares costs GHS 15 in commission.

## SCM Fee Schedule: Bond Investing

SCM charges a 0.5% fee on the face value when you purchase a bond. There is no fee for bond redemption at maturity — your principal is returned in full. Coupon payments are also processed at no charge. For example, purchasing a Ghana Government Treasury Note with GHS 10,000 face value costs GHS 50 in fees.

## SCM Fee Schedule: Account and Other Fees

SCM does not charge account maintenance fees or inactivity fees. The following additional fees may apply.

Currency Conversion: 1.0% when trading an instrument in a currency different from your wallet (for example, GHS to NGN).
Dividend Processing: Free, credited automatically.
Coupon Payment Processing: Free, credited automatically.
Physical Statement or Certificate: GHS 10 on request. Digital statements are free.

## Questions About Deposits at SCM

### How do I deposit money into my SCM account?

Go to Funds then Deposit in the SCM Invest app, choose your payment method, enter the amount, and confirm. Mobile money deposits (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money) are instant and free. Bank transfers take 1-2 business days.

### Can I deposit via mobile money at SCM?

Yes. Mobile money is the fastest way to fund your SCM account. We support MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. Go to Funds then Deposit then Mobile Money, select your provider, enter the amount (minimum GHS 50), and approve the prompt on your phone. The deposit is instant and free.

### Why is my deposit not showing in my SCM account?

Mobile money deposits should appear within minutes. Bank transfers in GHS take 1-2 business days. Cheque deposits take 5-7 business days. Check Funds then History in the SCM Invest app for the status of your deposit. If mobile money funds were debited but not credited within 1 hour, contact Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager).

### Can I deposit in US dollars at SCM?

Yes. You can fund your SCM account via international wire transfer in USD, EUR, or GBP. There is a $15 flat fee and the funds are converted to GHS at the market rate plus 1.0% conversion fee, unless you are purchasing a USD-denominated bond directly.

## Questions About Withdrawals at SCM

### I sold shares but cannot withdraw the money from SCM. Why?

When you sell shares on SCM, the proceeds are not available immediately due to the settlement period. GSE and BRVM trades settle in T+3 (3 business days after the sale). NSE trades settle in T+2. Once settlement is complete, the funds move to your available balance and can be withdrawn. Check your trade date under Portfolio then Trade History to calculate when settlement completes.

### Is there a maximum withdrawal amount at SCM?

There is no hard maximum, but withdrawals above GHS 50,000 may require additional compliance verification by Emeka Nwosu (Head of Compliance) and take longer to process.

### How many times can I withdraw with mobile money at SCM?

You can withdraw with mobile money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo) as many times as you want. There is no limit on the number of withdrawals. The first 2 mobile money withdrawals each month are free. From the 3rd withdrawal onwards, a GHS 2 fee applies per withdrawal. All mobile money withdrawals are processed same-day.

### Can I withdraw to a different bank account or mobile money number?

For security, your first withdrawal from SCM must go to a payment method you have previously used for a deposit. To add a new withdrawal destination, go to Settings then Payment Methods in the SCM Invest app and complete the verification process.

## Questions About Fees at SCM

### What is the currency conversion fee at SCM?

If your SCM account is in GHS and you buy shares on the NSE (priced in NGN) or the BRVM (priced in XOF), a 1.0% currency conversion fee applies. You can avoid this by adding a multi-currency wallet in the relevant currency under Settings then Currency Wallets in the SCM Invest app.

### Are there any hidden fees or inactivity fees at SCM?

No. SCM does not charge inactivity fees or account maintenance fees. All fees are published. The main costs are the 1.5% equity trading commission (minimum GHS 5) and the 0.5% bond purchase fee. Currency conversion is 1.0% when applicable.

### How are dividends paid at SCM?

When a company you hold shares in declares a dividend, the cash is automatically credited to your SCM account on the payment date, provided you held the shares on the ex-dividend date. There is no fee for dividend processing. Check the payment date in the weekly West Africa Market Pulse report from Dr. Nana Adjei (Head of Research).

### How are bond coupon payments handled at SCM?

Coupon payments from bonds you hold are automatically credited to your SCM account on the scheduled payment dates. For example, if you hold the Ghana Government 3-Year Treasury Note with an 18% coupon, the interest is paid semi-annually. There is no fee for coupon processing.

## Issue: Mobile Money Deposit Not Credited to SCM Account

If your mobile money deposit (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, or AirtelTigo Money) was debited from your wallet but not credited to your SCM investment account, common causes include the transaction failing on the mobile money provider's side, an incorrect SCM merchant code, or a network timeout during the approval step.

Steps to resolve: Check your mobile money transaction history to confirm the payment was debited and note the transaction ID. Check Funds then History in SCM Invest for the deposit status.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if funds were debited from your mobile money wallet but not credited to your SCM account within 1 hour. Provide the mobile money transaction ID.

## Issue: Sale Proceeds Not Available for Withdrawal from SCM

If you sold shares on SCM but the proceeds are not available for withdrawal, this is likely due to the settlement period. GSE and BRVM equities settle in T+3 (3 business days). NSE equities settle in T+2. Proceeds are locked until settlement is complete.

Steps to resolve: Check the trade date under Portfolio then Trade History. Count the settlement days (T+3 for GSE/BRVM, T+2 for NSE) excluding weekends and public holidays. Once settlement completes, funds appear in your available balance.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the settlement period has passed and the funds are still not available.

## Issue: Dividend Not Received in SCM Account

If a dividend payment has not appeared in your SCM account, you may not have held the shares on the ex-dividend date, the company may not have reached the payment date yet, or the dividend may still be processing.

Steps to resolve: Confirm that you held the shares before the ex-dividend date by checking your purchase date under Portfolio then Holdings. Check the dividend payment date on the company's announcement or in the weekly West Africa Market Pulse report.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the payment date has passed and the dividend has not been credited to your SCM account.

## Issue: Unexpected Fee Charged on SCM Account

If you see an unexpected fee on your SCM account, common causes include a currency conversion fee from trading in a different currency, an equity commission on a recent trade, or a physical statement request fee.

Steps to resolve: Check the fee description under Funds then History then Fees in the SCM Invest app. Compare against the SCM fee schedule. For currency conversion fees, check whether the trade was in a currency different from your wallet.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the fee does not match any published rate or you believe it was applied in error.

## Issue: Bank Transfer Deposit Not Credited to SCM Account

If your bank transfer deposit has not been credited to your SCM account after the expected processing time (1-2 business days for GHS, 2-3 business days for NGN or international wire), common causes include an incorrect reference number, bank processing delays, or the transfer being sent from an account not in your name.

Steps to resolve: Check Funds then History in SCM Invest for deposit status. Verify the reference number matches the one provided by SCM. Confirm the transfer was sent from a bank account in your name. Contact your bank to confirm the transfer was completed.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if funds have left your bank but are not reflected in your SCM account after the maximum processing time.

## Issue: Withdrawal Request Stuck in Processing at SCM

If your SCM withdrawal request has been in Processing status for an extended time, common causes include a pending compliance review for large amounts, KYC re-verification required, or the withdrawal amount exceeding your available balance due to unsettled trades.

Steps to resolve: Check Funds then History for status messages. Ensure your KYC is fully up to date. Verify that your available balance (not total balance) covers the withdrawal amount. Wait for any unsettled trades to complete before retrying.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the withdrawal has been in Processing for more than 5 business days. For large withdrawals above GHS 50,000, contact Emeka Nwosu (Head of Compliance).

## Escalation and Support Routing for Deposit and Withdrawal Issues

When escalating a deposit, withdrawal, or fee issue at SCM, collect the following before contacting the team: transaction reference or mobile money transaction ID, amount and currency, payment method used, date of the transaction, and screenshots of bank statements or mobile money receipts if available.

Route to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager): Failed deposits, stuck withdrawals, mobile money deposit issues, settlement timing queries, fee disputes.

Route to Emeka Nwosu (Head of Compliance): Large withdrawal reviews above GHS 50,000, re-verification requests, account-level payment restrictions.
