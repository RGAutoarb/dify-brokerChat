# Trading Products and Order Execution — Sahel Capital Markets (SCM)

## Overview

This document covers the financial products available through Sahel Capital Markets (SCM) and how orders are placed and executed on the SCM Invest platform. SCM offers 20 selected West African equities and a curated menu of sovereign and corporate bonds across the Ghana Stock Exchange (GSE), Nigerian Stock Exchange (NSE), and Bourse Regionale (BRVM). If you want to understand what you can invest in and how trading works, start here.

## Key Trading Concepts at SCM

### What Are Equities (Shares)?

An equity or share is a unit of ownership in a publicly traded West African company. When you buy shares in a company through SCM, you own a small piece of that company and may receive dividends. SCM offers 20 selected equities across banking, telecoms, mining, agriculture, energy, and consumer goods sectors on the GSE, NSE, and BRVM.

### What Are Bonds?

A bond is a fixed-income security where you lend money to a government or corporation through SCM. In return, you receive regular coupon (interest) payments and your principal back at maturity. SCM offers both sovereign bonds (issued by governments like Ghana and Nigeria) and corporate bonds (issued by companies like Ecobank and Sonatel).

### Coupon Rate and Maturity Date

The coupon rate is the annual interest rate paid on a bond, expressed as a percentage of face value. For example, an 18% coupon on a GHS 1,000 bond pays GHS 180 per year. The maturity date is when the bond's principal (face value) is repaid to you. Until maturity, you receive regular coupon payments on the scheduled dates.

### Subscription Window and Current Bond Menu

A subscription window is a limited period during which a new bond offering can be purchased through SCM. Once the window closes, the bond is no longer available for primary purchase, though it may trade on the secondary market. The Current Bond Menu is SCM's monthly-updated list of available bonds, published in-app and via email.

### Order Types on SCM

A Market Order buys or sells shares immediately at the best available price. Execution is fast but the exact price is not guaranteed. A Limit Order buys or sells shares only at a specific price or better. The price is guaranteed but execution is not — the order stays pending until the market reaches your price. A Stop-Loss Order automatically sells your shares if the price drops to a specified level, used to limit losses. Stop-loss orders apply to equities only, not bonds.

### Lot Size and Ex-Dividend Date

Lot size is the minimum tradeable unit on some exchanges. On the NSE, some equities trade in lots of 100 shares. On the GSE and BRVM, you can buy as few as 1 share. The ex-dividend date is the cutoff date to be eligible for a dividend payment. You must hold shares before this date to receive the dividend.

### Settlement at SCM

Settlement is the process of finalizing a trade. After you buy or sell shares through SCM, settlement takes T+3 on the GSE and BRVM (3 business days) or T+2 on the NSE (2 business days) before ownership officially transfers and sale proceeds become available for withdrawal.

## How to Place an Equity Trade on SCM

Follow these steps to buy or sell shares on the SCM Invest platform.

1. Log in to your verified and funded SCM Invest account.
2. Browse equities by exchange (GSE, NSE, or BRVM) or by sector using the Markets tab.
3. Select the equity to open its detail page showing current price, daily change, and trading volume.
4. Tap Buy or Sell.
5. Choose your order type: Market Order or Limit Order.
6. Enter the number of shares. Minimum is 1 share on GSE and BRVM. NSE equities may have lot size requirements.
7. Optionally set a Stop-Loss level to automatically sell if the price drops.
8. Review the order summary including the estimated commission of 1.5% of trade value (minimum GHS 5).
9. Tap Confirm to submit the order.
10. Market orders execute during trading hours. Limit orders remain pending until the target price is reached or you cancel them.

### Equity Trade Example on SCM

You want to buy 500 shares of Consolidated Bank Ghana (CBG) on the GSE at the current market price of GHS 8.50 per share. Your order value is GHS 4,250. The SCM commission is 1.5% which equals GHS 63.75. Total cost is GHS 4,313.75. Settlement occurs in T+3 (3 business days), after which the shares appear in your portfolio.

## How to Purchase a Bond on SCM

Follow these steps to buy a bond on the SCM Invest platform.

1. Go to Markets then Bonds or check the Current Bond Menu for available offerings.
2. Select the bond to see its coupon rate, maturity date, payment schedule, currency, and minimum investment.
3. Tap Subscribe for new offerings during a subscription window, or Buy for bonds on the secondary market.
4. Enter the face value amount you wish to invest (must meet the minimum for that bond).
5. Review the order summary including the 0.5% purchase fee.
6. Tap Confirm to submit the order.
7. The bond is added to your portfolio on the settlement date. Coupons are credited automatically. At maturity, your principal is returned.

### Bond Purchase Example on SCM

You subscribe to the Ghana Government 3-Year Treasury Note with a face value of GHS 10,000 through SCM. The coupon rate is 18% paid semi-annually, so you receive GHS 900 every 6 months. The SCM purchase fee is 0.5% which equals GHS 50. At maturity after 3 years, your GHS 10,000 principal is returned in full at no charge.

## How to Sell a Bond Before Maturity on SCM

Some bonds can be sold on the secondary market before maturity through SCM, subject to availability of buyers. Go to Portfolio then Holdings then Bonds, select the bond, and tap Sell. The sale price depends on current market conditions and may be above or below the face value. Not all bonds have active secondary markets — less liquid bonds may need to be held to maturity.

## Trading Hours on SCM

SCM routes orders to three West African stock exchanges. Each has different trading hours. Orders placed outside trading hours are queued and executed when the relevant exchange opens.

Ghana Stock Exchange (GSE): Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 3:00 PM GMT.
Nigerian Stock Exchange (NSE): Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 2:30 PM WAT which is GMT+1, equivalent to 8:30 AM to 1:30 PM GMT.
Bourse Regionale (BRVM): Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM GMT.

Bond subscriptions during a subscription window can be placed at any time and are processed on the issue date.

## Questions About Orders and Trading Hours on SCM

### What are the trading hours for Nigerian stocks on SCM?

NSE trading hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 2:30 PM WAT (which is GMT+1, equivalent to 8:30 AM to 1:30 PM GMT). Orders placed outside these hours through SCM are queued for the next trading session.

### Why is the BRVM price shown in XOF on SCM?

BRVM-listed equities are denominated in West African CFA Francs (XOF). If your SCM account base currency is GHS, the platform converts the cost at the current exchange rate plus 1.0% conversion fee. You can avoid this fee by adding an XOF wallet under Settings then Currency Wallets.

### What does pending order mean on SCM?

A pending order (limit order) on SCM has been submitted but not yet executed. It remains active until the market reaches your target price, or until you cancel it. Limit orders on the GSE, NSE, and BRVM are valid for the current trading day unless you specify otherwise.

## Issue: Order Rejected Due to Market Closed on SCM

If your order was rejected with a market closed message on SCM, you likely placed a market order outside of trading hours for that exchange. The GSE, NSE, and BRVM each have different hours, and the NSE operates in WAT (GMT+1) not GMT.

Steps to resolve: Check the trading hours section above for the relevant exchange. Convert to your local time if needed. Submit the order during market hours, or use a limit order which will queue until the next session.

Escalate to Yaw Boateng (Head Trader) if the order is rejected during what should be market hours.

## Issue: Cannot Find a Bond on SCM

If you cannot find a specific bond on the SCM Invest platform, the subscription window for that bond may have closed, the bond may not be on the current month's Bond Menu, or the bond may have already matured.

Steps to resolve: Check the Current Bond Menu under Markets then Bonds for available offerings. Check your email for the latest bond availability notification from SCM. If a specific bond is not listed, the subscription window may have closed or it may not yet be available.

Escalate to Fatima Diallo (Bond Desk Manager) if you believe a bond should be available but cannot find it on SCM.

## Issue: Order Partially Filled on SCM

If your equity order on SCM was only partially filled, this is typically due to low trading volume (liquidity) on some GSE or BRVM equities. Your order was larger than the available volume at your price level.

Steps to resolve: Check Portfolio then Trade History for the fill details. The unfilled portion remains as a pending order if it was a limit order. For less liquid stocks such as mining or agriculture equities, consider using smaller order sizes or wider limit prices. Banking and telecoms equities generally have higher liquidity.

Escalate to Yaw Boateng (Head Trader) if the partial fill seems incorrect or the pending portion was cancelled without your instruction.

## Issue: Dividend Not Received for Shares Held on SCM

If you have not received a dividend for shares you hold on SCM, you may have purchased shares after the ex-dividend date, the company may not have reached the payment date, or the dividend may still be processing.

Steps to resolve: Confirm you held the shares before the ex-dividend date by checking your purchase date under Portfolio then Holdings. Check the dividend payment date in the company announcement or the weekly West Africa Market Pulse report from Dr. Nana Adjei.

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the payment date has passed and the dividend has not been credited to your SCM account.

## Issue: Equity Order Rejected Due to Insufficient Funds on SCM

If your equity order on SCM was rejected with an insufficient funds message, your available balance may be too low for the trade value plus the 1.5% commission, recent deposits may not yet be credited, or sale proceeds from a recent trade may not have settled yet.

Steps to resolve: Check your available balance under Portfolio then Account Summary in SCM Invest. Remember that the 1.5% commission (minimum GHS 5) is charged on top of the trade value. Check if recent deposits are confirmed under Funds then History. If you recently sold shares, proceeds may not have settled (T+2 for NSE, T+3 for GSE/BRVM).

Escalate to Kofi Mensah (Operations Manager) if the balance shown appears incorrect.
