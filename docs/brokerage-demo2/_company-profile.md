## Company Identity

- **Legal Name:** Sahel Capital Markets Limited
- **Trading Name:** SCM
- **Tagline:** "Your Gateway to West African Growth"
- **Founded:** 2012
- **Headquarters:** Ridge Tower, 14 Liberation Road, Accra, Ghana
- **Regional Offices:** Lagos, Nigeria | Abidjan, Cote d'Ivoire
- **Regulators:** Securities and Exchange Commission of Ghana (SEC Ghana); Nigeria Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC Nigeria); Conseil Regional de l'Epargne Publique et des Marches Financiers (CREPMF) for BRVM/UEMOA markets
- **Platform:** SCM Invest (web portal and mobile app for iOS and Android)
- **Mission:** To connect everyday investors with opportunities in West Africa's fastest-growing economies through transparent, accessible equity and bond investing.
- **What we sell:** Selected West African equities (20 listings) and curated bond offerings (sovereign and corporate). No forex, no CFDs, no options, no cryptocurrency.

## Settlement and Custody

- **Equity settlement (GSE):** T+3 (trade date plus 3 business days)
- **Equity settlement (BRVM):** T+3
- **Equity settlement (NSE):** T+2
- **Bond settlement (secondary market):** T+2
- **Bond settlement (primary subscription):** Settles on issue date
- **Custodian for Ghanaian securities:** Ghana Central Securities Depository (CSD)
- **Custodian for Nigerian securities:** Central Securities Clearing System (CSCS)
- **Custodian for BRVM securities:** Depositaire Central / Banque de Reglement (DC/BR)
- **Client funds:** Held in segregated trust accounts at licensed commercial banks, separate from SCM's operating accounts

- **Base account currency:** Ghana Cedis (GHS)
