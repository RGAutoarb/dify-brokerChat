# Accounts and Onboarding — Sahel Capital Markets (SCM)

## Overview

This document covers account types, account opening steps, and identity verification (KYC) at Sahel Capital Markets (SCM). SCM is a West African brokerage offering 20 selected equities and curated bond offerings across the Ghana Stock Exchange (GSE), Nigerian Stock Exchange (NSE), and Bourse Regionale (BRVM). If you are looking to open a new investment account or understand account types, start here.

## Account Types at SCM

SCM offers four account types for investing in West African equities and bonds. There is no margin trading or leverage — you invest only with the funds you deposit.

### Individual Investment Account

The standard SCM account for buying and selling equities and bonds on the SCM Invest platform. You trade using only the funds you deposit. There is no borrowing or leverage. This is the most common account type for personal investors. Minimum opening deposit is GHS 500.

### Joint Account

A shared investment account for two individuals, commonly used by family members or business partners investing through SCM. Both account holders must complete KYC verification. Either holder can place trades on equities and bonds, but withdrawals require consent from both parties. Minimum opening deposit is GHS 500.

### Corporate and Institutional Account

An account for registered businesses, trusts, or organizations investing through SCM. Requires business registration documents from the Registrar General's Department and a board resolution authorizing the account. Suitable for companies investing treasury funds or institutional investors accessing West African markets. Minimum opening deposit is GHS 500.

### Demo Account

A practice account with virtual funds so you can explore the SCM Invest platform and learn how equity and bond investing works without risking real money. Demo accounts simulate real market prices from the GSE, NSE, and BRVM with a 15-minute delay. No deposit required.

## KYC Verification at SCM

KYC (Know Your Customer) is a legal requirement for SCM to verify your identity before you can deposit or invest. This protects you, prevents fraud, and satisfies SEC Ghana, SEC Nigeria, and CREPMF regulatory requirements.

### Accepted KYC Documents

SCM accepts the following documents for identity verification when opening an investment account.

Government-issued photo ID (one required): Ghana Card, ECOWAS Passport, Ghana Voter ID, Nigeria National Identification Number (NIN) slip, or Cote d'Ivoire National ID.

Proof of address dated within 3 months (one required): Utility bill (ECG, Ghana Water, GWCL), bank statement, SSNIT statement, or tenancy agreement.

Additional documents for Corporate accounts: Certificate of Incorporation from Registrar General, board resolution, and Tax Identification Number (TIN).

### KYC Processing Times

Ghana Card holders are typically verified same-day via the automated GhanaCard API integration. Other documents are reviewed manually and take 1-2 business days. Complex cases such as international documents may take up to 3 business days. You cannot deposit funds or place trades until KYC is complete, but you can explore the platform using a Demo Account in the meantime.

## How to Open an Individual Investment Account

Follow these steps to open a personal investment account on SCM Invest for trading West African equities and bonds.

1. Download the SCM Invest app or visit the SCM Invest web portal.
2. Enter your full legal name, email address, mobile phone number, and create a secure password.
3. Select Individual Investment Account as your account type.
4. Provide your date of birth, nationality, residential address, and occupation.
5. Upload identity documents for KYC verification.
6. Answer a short suitability questionnaire about your investment experience.
7. Review and accept the terms of service, risk disclosures, and privacy policy.
8. Wait for KYC review.
9. Once approved, fund your account with a minimum deposit of GHS 500 and begin investing.

## How to Open a Joint Account

Follow these steps to open a shared investment account for two individuals on SCM Invest.

1. One partner registers on SCM Invest and selects Joint Account during sign-up.
2. Enter the personal details and KYC documents for both account holders.
3. Both holders must individually verify their identity (both must pass KYC).
4. Once both holders are verified, the account is activated.
5. Fund the account with a minimum deposit of GHS 500.

Either holder can place trades on equities and bonds. Withdrawals require consent from both parties.

## How to Add Multi-Currency Wallets

By default, your SCM account is denominated in Ghana Cedis (GHS). If you want to trade Nigerian equities on the NSE or BRVM-listed equities without paying the 1.0% currency conversion fee on every trade, you can add NGN and XOF sub-wallets.

1. Go to Settings then Currency Wallets in the SCM Invest app.
2. Select Add NGN Wallet or Add XOF Wallet.
3. Fund the wallet directly via bank transfer in that currency, or convert from your GHS balance.
4. When placing trades on the NSE or BRVM, the platform draws from the matching currency wallet automatically.

## Questions About Opening an SCM Account

### What is the minimum deposit to open an SCM account?

The minimum deposit is GHS 500 (approximately 40 USD) for all account types — Individual, Joint, and Corporate. Demo accounts do not require a deposit.

### Can I have more than one SCM account?

Yes. You can hold one Individual account, one Joint account, and one Demo account under the same email address. Corporate accounts are registered separately with business documents.

### Can non-Ghanaians open an SCM account?

Yes. SCM welcomes investors from all ECOWAS member states and beyond. You will need a valid passport and proof of address. Non-ECOWAS nationals may require additional documentation and a longer KYC review period.

### What is the difference between SCM account types?

An Individual account is for personal investing. A Joint account is shared between two people with joint consent for withdrawals. A Corporate account is for businesses and institutions. All three account types give you access to the same 20 West African equities and curated bond offerings.

### Do I need a Tax Identification Number to open an SCM account?

A TIN is required only for Corporate and Institutional accounts. Individual and Joint accounts use your Ghana Card number, NIN, or passport number as your primary identifier.

### What equities and bonds can I invest in through SCM?

SCM offers 20 selected West African equities listed on the GSE, NSE, and BRVM across banking, telecoms, mining, agriculture, energy, and consumer goods sectors. SCM also offers a curated menu of sovereign and corporate bonds. See the Trading Products document for the full list.

### Is my Demo Account balance real money?

No. Demo accounts use virtual funds for practice only. Profits and losses are simulated. Market prices are from the GSE, NSE, and BRVM with a 15-minute delay.

## Issue: KYC Verification Rejected

If your KYC verification was rejected when opening an SCM account, common causes include a blurry or expired document, a name mismatch between your ID and the name entered during registration, or an unsupported document type.

Steps to resolve: Check the rejection reason in your notification email or under Settings then Verification Status. Re-upload a clear, valid document. Ensure the name on your ID matches the name on your account exactly, including middle names.

Escalate to Abena Owusu (KYC Officer) if verification fails after two re-submissions, or if you believe the rejection is an error.

## Issue: Cannot Log In to SCM Account

If you cannot log in to your SCM Invest account, common causes include an incorrect password, account locked after multiple failed attempts, email not confirmed, or mobile number changed.

Steps to resolve: Use the Forgot Password link to reset via email. Check your email including spam for a confirmation link. Wait 30 minutes if the account is temporarily locked due to failed login attempts.

Escalate to Ama Mensah (Head of Client Relations, Accra) or Chidi Okonkwo (Client Relations Manager, Lagos) if you still cannot log in after resetting your password and confirming your email.

## Issue: Mobile Number Verification Failed

If the OTP (one-time password) for mobile verification is not arriving when opening your SCM account, common causes include the mobile number not being registered in your name with the telecom provider, a recent SIM card change, or network issues.

Steps to resolve: Ensure the number you entered is registered to your name with your mobile provider (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo). Wait 2 minutes and request a new OTP. Check that you have network signal and your SMS inbox is not full. Try the voice call OTP option if SMS is not working.

Escalate to Ama Mensah (Head of Client Relations) if OTP delivery fails repeatedly.

## Issue: Corporate Account Application Delayed

If your Corporate or Institutional account application at SCM is taking longer than expected, common causes include missing or incomplete business registration documents, a board resolution not signed by all required directors, or a TIN not yet issued.

Steps to resolve: Check the status under Settings then Verification Status for a list of pending documents. Ensure the Certificate of Incorporation is from the Registrar General's Department and is current. Submit a board resolution signed by at least two directors authorizing the opening of the investment account.

Escalate to Emeka Nwosu (Head of Compliance) if all documents have been submitted and the application has been pending for more than 5 business days.

## Issue: Incorrect Personal Information on SCM Account

If your SCM account shows incorrect personal information such as a typo during registration or an outdated address after a move.

Steps to resolve: Go to Settings then Personal Information in SCM Invest. Update address, phone number, or email directly. Name or date of birth changes require submitting a support request with an updated ID document.

Escalate to Abena Owusu (KYC Officer) for any request to change the legal name or date of birth on the account.

## Escalation and Support Routing for Account Issues

When escalating an SCM account or onboarding issue, collect the following before contacting the team: full name on account, registered email address and mobile number, description of the issue, any error messages or rejection reasons received, and screenshots if available.

Route to Abena Owusu (KYC Officer): Repeated verification failures, document disputes, sanctions screening issues, name change requests.

Route to Ama Mensah (Head of Client Relations, Accra) or Chidi Okonkwo (Client Relations Manager, Lagos): Login issues unresolved after password reset, account closure requests, general account queries.

Route to Marie Kouassi (Client Relations Associate, Abidjan): BRVM-related queries, Francophone client support.

Route to Emeka Nwosu (Head of Compliance): Regulatory holds, compliance reviews, corporate account delays.
